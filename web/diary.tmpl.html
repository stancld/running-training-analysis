The 29 weeks

Running hours against what actually happened. The plan is written week by week only inside an eight-week horizon; beyond it each phase carries a band rather than a number, because a specific hour target four months out is precision the evidence cannot support — and scoring compliance against it would be worse than useless. The bands narrow into real weeks as each block comes inside the horizon.

Every month since 2023

Running hours per calendar month, shaded by volume. The gaps are the injuries: February–March 2024, April 2025, and the two months the January 2026 calf cost. Seasons are calendar years, the way Dan counts them.

Week ledger

Every week, scored against the seven guardrails. Week-over-week ratio and ACWR are running hours only — the bike carries load the tendons never see, which is the whole reason it is in the plan.

How to read the flags. R1 a week-over-week jump above 1.25×, the single strongest injury predictor in the record. R2 acute:chronic above 1.30. R3 more than 8 h of running below 300 m of climb per hour — flat volume, the mechanism behind the calf and shin failures. R6 over the 100 km ceiling. R4 fires only for flat interval or repetition work; uphill reps carry the intent of the rule and are not flagged.
